Questions I usually get

Do I need a new GoHighLevel account?

No. I work inside the account you already have. If you don't have one yet, I can set it up from scratch.

What if the AI says something wrong to a lead?

The AI works from your own answers and rules, hands the conversation to a person when it's unsure, and sensitive messages can require approval before they go out.

Will my texts get blocked?

I set daily caps, opt-out handling and delivery monitoring, and I check your A2P registration. Carriers have the last word, but most blocks come from things that can be fixed.

How long until it's live?

The audit takes a few days. The instant response and the first follow-up usually go live in one to two weeks, depending on your account.

How does payment work?

Everything goes through Upwork, hourly or with a fixed first step. You see the hours and the work, and payments are protected by Upwork.

What happens after the build?

You keep it with the documentation, or I keep running it with weekly checks on delivery, replies and bookings.
